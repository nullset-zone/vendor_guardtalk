01 — The problem

Two operating systems on one screen.

As shipped, the GuardTalk apps sit on obsidian while every stock utility is a white Android circle. The eye sorts them into two products. The fix isn’t to paint everything green — the brand book keeps Signal Green to roughly a tenth of any surface. It’s to give the utilities the same obsidian console ground and monoline language, and let the GuardTalk apps stay the ones that glow.

02 — The system

Two tiers, one shell.

Every icon shares the ground: obsidian, a quiet HUD grid, a single corner glow. What changes is the glyph treatment — and that difference does real work, sorting the tray into the apps you secure with and the tools you secure.

Tier 1 · System utilities

Soft-white monoline glyph + one green accent. Camera, Clock, Contacts, Files, Gallery, PDF Viewer, Settings. Clearly GuardTalkOS, never competing with the heroes. The accent is always a single, meaningful spark.

Tier 2 · GuardTalk apps

Signal-Green glyph, shield DNA. GT Config and GT Info carry the chevron mark and the full green. These are the apps the brand exists for — so they’re the ones that read first.

03 — Build

Shipped the same way.

Identical construction to the GuardTalk app icons: two-layer Android adaptive icons plus pre-masked launcher sizes. This device masks to circles, so circle is the default export — squircle and rounded are included for other launchers.
